* Full Description (Formatted for Google Play Console):
Never get caught with a ringing phone in class, library, or exam hall again!

BlockSilent is a battery-efficient, privacy-first Android application that automatically switches your phone's sound profile (Silent, Vibrate, Normal) based on your real-time campus locations and weekly academic timetable.

🌟 KEY FEATURES:
• True Background Geofencing: Powered by Android Location Services. Detects location boundaries even when the app is closed or your phone screen is locked.
• Dynamic Sound Automation: Automatically applies SILENT in lecture halls/exam halls, VIBRATE in computer labs, and restores NORMAL when you step outside.
• Smart Timetable Schedule: Integrates with your weekly subject schedule so your sound profile automatically syncs with active class hours.
• Priority Resolution: Intelligently handles overlapping locations (Exam Hall > Classroom > Lab > Library).
• Zero Cloud Tracking & 100% Private: All block coordinates, schedules, and logs remain strictly on your device in a secure local database.
• Battery Conscious: Uses event-driven geofences rather than continuous GPS polling to preserve your battery throughout the day.
• Emergency Override & DND Priority: Instantly pause automation for 1–4 hours or whitelist critical contacts.

📍 REQUIRED PERMISSIONS & WHY:
• Location (Foreground & Background): To detect when you enter or leave configured college blocks.
• Do Not Disturb Access: Required by Android system to safely change ringer mode to Silent.
• Notifications: To inform you when a sound mode transition occurs.
