Privacy First Architecture

BlockSilent Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Introduction & Commitment

BlockSilent ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. We believe that privacy is a fundamental human right.

Our Core Privacy Promise: BlockSilent operates 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any of your location data, schedules, contacts, or sound preferences to external servers or third parties.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Because the app operates on an offline-first, local-only architecture:

  • No Location Tracking Logs: Your GPS coordinates and geofence locations never leave your device.
  • No User Accounts: No registration, login, email address, or personal profile is required.
  • No Analytics Telemetry: We do not track user behavior or usage patterns.
  • No Advertising Identifiers: The application contains zero advertisements and zero ad-tracking SDKs.

3. Android Permissions & How They Are Used

BlockSilent requires specific Android permissions exclusively to perform automated location-based sound adjustments on your device. Each permission is used solely for its documented purpose:

Permission Technical Identifier Purpose & Necessity
Foreground Location ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION Required to accurately pinpoint campus blocks and display your location on the setup map.
Background Location ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION Allows Android Location Services to trigger sound mode changes when you enter or leave a block while your screen is locked or the app is minimized.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Used on Android 13+ to notify you when your sound mode has transitioned (e.g., from Normal to Silent).
Do Not Disturb Access ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY Required by Android system security to allow the app to adjust the ringer state to Silent mode.
Device Reboot RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Re-registers your saved geofence locations automatically when your device restarts so automation remains active.

4. Data Storage & Security

All application data—including block names, coordinates, timetable schedules, and transition history—is stored exclusively inside your device's sandboxed local SQLite database (powered by Android Room).

This data is private to BlockSilent and cannot be accessed by other applications on standard Android devices.

5. Third-Party Services

The application integrates with official Google Play Services solely for:

  • Google Maps SDK: To display maps when selecting coordinates. Google Maps handles map tiles according to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Geofencing API: System-level geofence boundary detection managed natively by Android OS.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can clear your activity history directly in the app at any time via Activity Log > Clear History.
  • Uninstalling BlockSilent from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes all local databases, preferences, and saved locations.

7. Children's Privacy

BlockSilent does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
