--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OBJECTIVE & PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BlockSilent is an intelligent, zero-cloud, context-aware sound automation system 
engineered specifically for academic campuses, libraries, laboratories, and 
structured workplace environments. 

The primary objective is to eliminate the embarrassment, disruption, and academic 
penalties of ringing or chiming phones during lectures, laboratory sessions, and 
formal examinations. 

Unlike standard sound profile schedulers that rely solely on static time-based clocks 
or battery-draining continuous GPS tracking, BlockSilent integrates hardware-level 
geofencing (Google Play Services), indoor Wi-Fi BSSID proximity detection, real-time 
device orientation sensors (Flip-to-Silence), and weekly academic timetables into a 
deterministic, hierarchical Priority Resolution Engine. Furthermore, it incorporates 
a life-safety "Emergency Break-Glass" VIP protocol to guarantee critical family 
calls always pierce through silent modes.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HIGH-LEVEL SYSTEM DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BlockSilent operates on a 100% offline-first, local-first philosophy. There are no 
remote tracking servers, analytics trackers, cloud databases, or background telemetry. 
All coordinates, timetables, VIP contact lists, and event logs remain strictly on 
the user's physical hardware within an encrypted SQLite/Room database.

The system continuously assesses contextual inputs from five independent triggers:
  1. GPS / Cellular Geofence Boundaries (Macro-location: Campus buildings)
  2. Wi-Fi Access Point BSSID Fingerprinting (Micro-location: Floors and Rooms)
  3. Device Orientation & Proximity Sensors (Physical context: Desk Face-Down)
  4. Weekly Academic Timetables (Temporal context: Subject & Class hours)
  5. VIP Emergency Overrides & Break-Glass SMS (Safety context: Urgent family alerts)

Whenever any environmental or temporal parameter changes, the unified Context Engine 
evaluates the state against a strict priority hierarchy and executes the appropriate 
hardware AudioManager transition (SILENT, VIBRATE, or NORMAL) within milliseconds.

3. CORE APP MODULES & TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The codebase is structured into modular layers designed for separation of concerns:

A. Database Layer (com.blocksilent.app.database)
   • AppDatabase: Thread-safe Room database instance with automated migrations.
   • BlockDao / BlockEntity: Campus geofence blocks (lat, lng, radius, sound mode, priority).
   • TimetableDao / TimetableEntity: Weekly recurring class slots tied to specific blocks.
   • ActiveGeofenceStateDao / ActiveGeofenceStateEntity: Real-time tracking of zone entries/exits.
   • HistoryDao / HistoryEntity: Full audit trail of transitions with timestamps and triggers.
   • EmergencyContactDao / EmergencyEventDao: VIP emergency whitelist and trigger history.
   • WifiZoneDao / WifiZoneEntity: Indoor Wi-Fi BSSID access point room/floor mappings.
   • SensorSettingsDao / SensorSettingsEntity: Thresholds for accelerometer and light sensors.
   • AutomationDecisionDao / AutomationDecisionEntity: Detailed logic reasoning records.
   • SettingsDao / SettingsEntity: Global automation toggles, notification preferences, override timers.

B. Context & Priority Arbitration Engine (com.blocksilent.app.context)
   • ContextEngine: The central singleton aggregator. Listens to sensor changes, geofence broadcasts, 
     Wi-Fi association changes, and timetable ticks. It compiles the current context state and 
     triggers the PriorityResolver.
   • PriorityResolver: Pure deterministic decision tree resolving conflicts between overlapping zones, 
     sensors, timers, and emergency overrides.
   • ContextState: Reactive data model exposing real-time status to the UI through LiveData.

C. Location & Geofencing Subsystem (com.blocksilent.app.geofence, services, utils)
   • GeofenceManager: Handles registration, updates, and unregistration of circular geofences 
     with Google Play Services LocationClient.
   • GeofenceBroadcastReceiver: Wakes on hardware boundary crossing with PendingIntent.
   • GeofenceEventProcessor: Validates transitions (ENTER, EXIT, DWELL) and applies a 6-second 
     debounce filter to eliminate perimeter bounce.
   • LocationMonitoringService: Foreground service for high-reliability location monitoring.
   • LocationHelper / PermissionManager: Manages fine location, coarse location, background 
     location permissions, and GPS availability checks.

D. Indoor Micro-Localization & Sensors (com.blocksilent.app.wifi, sensors, noise)
   • WifiZoneManager: Matches connected Wi-Fi SSID and BSSID against room-specific mappings.
   • SensorContextEngine: Evaluates tri-axis accelerometer (Z-axis gravity < -7.0 m/s²), 
     proximity sensor (< 2 cm), and ambient light (< 25 lux) to detect table flip-down gestures.
   • NoiseDetector: Zero-storage, in-memory RMS acoustic sensor. Calculates ambient sound pressure 
     (dB) without recording or retaining audio data.

E. VIP Emergency Break-Glass Subsystem (com.blocksilent.app.emergency)
   • EmergencyManager: Manages high-priority override states, siren playback, and contact cooldowns.
   • EmergencySmsReceiver: BroadcastReceiver monitoring incoming SMS for configured emergency 
     keywords ("EMERGENCY") from whitelisted numbers.
   • CallStateReceiver: Monitors repeated missed calls from VIP contacts to trigger override.

F. Presentation & UI Layer (com.blocksilent.app.activities, fragments, adapters)
   • Material Design 3 interactive UI with custom cards, badges, status meters, and animations.

4. END-TO-END APPLICATION FLOW & LIFECYCLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Boot & Cold Launch:
   • Device Boot -> BootCompletedReceiver wakes -> Reads active blocks from Room DB -> 
     Re-registers all geofences with Google Play Services.
   • User clicks App Icon -> SplashActivity displays branding -> Reads SettingsEntity.
   • If first launch (isFirstTimeLaunch == true) -> Navigates to OnboardingActivity.
   • If setup complete -> Navigates to MainActivity (Dashboard).

2. Background Execution & State Maintenance:
   • The app does not need to remain open in the foreground.
   • When the user walks into a campus block:
     a. Android OS Location Subsystem triggers GeofenceBroadcastReceiver.
     b. Receiver passes event to GeofenceEventProcessor in a background thread.
     c. Event processor updates ActiveGeofenceStateEntity in Room DB.
     d. ContextEngine gathers active geofences, Wi-Fi BSSID, and current Timetable slot.
     e. PriorityResolver selects winning sound mode.
     f. SoundModeManager applies AudioManager settings (e.g., RINGER_MODE_SILENT).
     g. WearableNotificationHelper dispatches a haptic alert to Wear OS devices & system shade.
     h. HistoryDao logs the event with timestamp and source explanation.

3. Exit & Restoration:
   • When the user exits the block:
     a. OS fires GEOFENCE_TRANSITION_EXIT.
     b. ActiveGeofenceState is cleared.
     c. ContextEngine detects no remaining active blocks or timetable slots.
     d. SoundModeManager restores previous sound mode (NORMAL/RING).

7. DETAILED OPERATIONAL WORKFLOWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Workflow 1: Geofence Entry & Sound Automation
1. Student enters Physics Lecture Hall (50m radius).
2. Android LocationClient fires GeofenceBroadcastReceiver with GEOFENCE_TRANSITION_ENTER.
3. Receiver calls GeofenceEventProcessor (verifies 6-second debounce).
4. Room DB records active entry in active_geofence_state.
5. ContextEngine is notified -> passes active state to PriorityResolver.
6. PriorityResolver checks:
   - Emergency active? No.
   - Manual pause? No.
   - Timetable active? Yes, "Physics 101" (Silent).
7. Decision resolved: SILENT mode.
8. SoundModeManager safely invokes AudioManager.setRingerMode(RINGER_MODE_SILENT).
9. Wearable helper fires silent wrist tap; notification banner updates.
10. History logged: "Entered Physics Lecture Hall -> Sound mode set to SILENT".

Workflow 2: Flip-to-Silence On Desk
1. Student sits in study library and places phone face-down on desk.
2. Accelerometer detects Z-axis < -7.0 m/s², Proximity detects < 2.0 cm, Light sensor < 25 lux.
3. SensorContextEngine initiates a 1500 ms confirmation timer.
4. After 1500 ms continuous flat orientation, onFaceDownSilenceStateChanged(true) fires.
5. ContextEngine activates SILENT mode (Priority 80).
6. When student picks up phone, Z-axis normalizes -> Pickup restored -> Sound mode restored in 500 ms.

Workflow 3: VIP Emergency Break-Glass SMS
1. Phone is in SILENT mode inside Exam Hall.
2. Mother sends SMS: "Where are you? EMERGENCY call me now".
3. EmergencySmsReceiver intercepts SMS broadcast.
4. Checks sender number against whitelisted EmergencyContactDao.
5. Identifies keyword "EMERGENCY" and confirms contact cooldown > 15 mins.
6. EmergencyManager triggers Break-Glass:
   - Sets temporary 2-hour manual override.
   - AudioManager immediately switches to NORMAL mode with 100% ring/alarm volume.
   - Starts looping audible emergency alarm siren.
   - UI displays full-screen flashing Emergency Active banner with "Acknowledge" button.
7. Student acknowledges alert -> Siren stops -> Automation remains paused until dismissed.

Workflow 4: System Reboot & Cold Recovery
1. Device battery dies or restarts.
2. Android OS broadcasts ACTION_BOOT_COMPLETED.
3. BootCompletedReceiver activates -> queries Room database for enabled blocks.
4. Re-registers all geofence requests with Google Play Services.
5. ContextEngine evaluates current time against TimetableDao to immediately restore correct sound state.

9. CORE BENEFITS & VALUE PROPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Guaranteed Peace of Mind: Students, professors, and professionals never need 
   to worry about forgetting to silence their phones before entering class or meetings.
2. Reliable Auto-Restoration: Phones automatically return to Normal ringer mode upon 
   leaving the campus block, preventing missed important calls after school hours.
3. Safety Without Compromise: The Emergency Break-Glass system ensures family members 
   can always reach users in true emergencies without leaving phones unsilenced all day.
4. Extreme Battery Efficiency: Uses OS-native hardware geofencing geocells instead of 
   continuous GPS polling, ensuring 0% noticeable battery impact.
5. Absolute Privacy: No location data, timetable schedules, or contacts ever leave 
   the user's physical device.
6. Works 100% Offline: Operates seamlessly in basement labs or underground lecture halls 
   without requiring active internet connectivity.
